XGAlbums

Albums, music and zoom for Instagram 6.

Install

Open in Cydia Add slopkit to Cydia

How to Use

  1. Open Instagram 6.5.1 signed in to your Xstragram server.
  2. Albums show a 1/4 badge: swipe sideways. Pinch any feed photo to zoom.

There are no settings to configure.

What it does

For Instagram 6.5.1 running on an Xstragram server.

  • Swipe through multi-photo posts, with a 1/4 badge.
  • An album's song plays while it's on screen.
  • Video slides play inside albums.
  • Pinch any feed photo to zoom it.
  • Feed videos play with sound without pressing a volume button.
