What it does

  • Choose Single, Double, Triple or iPhone Default for texts, notifications and apps, reminders, the ring/silent switch, charging and calls.
  • Strength, tap length and gap are adjustable, with a preview as you slide.
  • Vibrations picked in Settings › Sounds can become taps too.
  • Works everywhere. Takes effect after a reboot.
