TickLock

A light tick when you unlock (and lock).

Install

Open in Cydia Add slopkit to Cydia

How to Use

  1. Unlock as usual: you feel one light tick.
  2. Settings › TickLock: tick on lock too, match KeyTick, or set your own strength and length.

What it does

  • One light tick on unlock: slide to unlock, a correct passcode, or opening a notification.
  • An optional tick on lock too, from the sleep button or Auto-Lock.
  • Same feel as KeyTick's key taps, using KeyTick's Strength and Length by default, or its own sliders.

Settings

Without slopkit Settings installed, it's at Settings › TickLock.
