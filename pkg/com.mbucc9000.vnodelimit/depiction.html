Vnode Limit

Stops iOS 6 closing your apps when it runs out of open files.

How to Use

  1. Install it. The new limit applies right away and at every boot.
  2. There's nothing to open or set. To undo it, remove Vnode Limit and reboot.

There are no settings to configure.

What it does

  • iOS 6 on the iPhone 5 only allows 1250 open files at once. With a lot of tweaks installed, a Cydia refresh can use them all, and iOS closes apps to free some, often Cydia itself.
  • Vnode Limit raises the limit to 3000 at every boot. That costs about 1 MB of memory.
  • Nothing to set up. Remove it and reboot to go back to the stock limit.
