What it does

  • AirPodsReconnectFix by mikey820 (commissioned): keeps the AirPods connected and the audio routed to them.
  • AirPodsIcon: an AirPods icon in place of the Bluetooth glyph while they're connected, no headset-battery gap, and no " - Find My" on the AirPods name in Settings and audio pickers.
  • AirPodsUnstick: fixes the audio going silent or choppy after the AirPods connect. The first time you press play, it does what Siri does (a one-second hop of the audio route), then your app carries on.
  • Name your AirPods anything: all parts recognise them by identity, not by the word "AirPods".
  • Replaces the three separate packages.
