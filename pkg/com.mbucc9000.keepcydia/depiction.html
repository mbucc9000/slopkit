KeepCydia

Cydia stays open in the background, like on iOS 8.

How to Use

  1. Open Cydia and let it load.
  2. Press Home. Cydia stays in the app switcher instead of quitting.
  3. Open Cydia again and it's right where you left it.

There are no settings to configure.

What it does

  • Press Home and Cydia stays in the background instead of quitting, so it opens right where you left it with no Reloading Data.
  • It switches on Cydia's own iOS 8 background mode: if packages change while it's away, Cydia reloads them when you come back, and after 30 minutes it goes back to the Home tab.
  • If a refresh is still running when you press Home, Cydia quits like it always did.
  • Comes with Vnode Limit, so a Cydia refresh can't run iOS 6 out of open files.
