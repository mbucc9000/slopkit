NewMail

Gmail-style tabs for Mail.

How to Use

  1. Install NewMail and open Mail. Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates and Forums appear under All Inboxes.
  2. Open a tab. Use the colored bar at the top to switch tabs, or tap the title to pick an account.
  3. Tap a thread to read the whole conversation, with your replies in it. Pull down to refresh.
  4. Hide tabs, or turn off Gmail's sorting, Conversation View, the tab bar or sender pictures, in Settings › NewMail.

What it does

  • Your inbox is sorted into Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates and Forums. Each one is a mailbox under All Inboxes with its own unread count, and a colored tab bar switches between them.
  • On Gmail accounts Gmail does the sorting, so it matches the Gmail app. Other accounts are sorted on this iPhone.
  • A thread opens as one conversation, like Gmail, with your own replies in it. Quoted text is folded away.
  • Select and copy text in any email.
  • Tap the title to show one account or all of them. Senders get colored letter pictures.
  • Turn each tab and extra on or off in Settings > NewMail. Removing NewMail puts Mail back the way it was.
