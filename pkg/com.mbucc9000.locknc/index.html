What it does

  • Swipe down from the top edge of the lock screen to open Notification Center, just like when unlocked.
  • Close it by swiping up from the bottom, pressing Home, or letting the screen sleep.
  • Tapping a notification while locked just closes it, so nothing opens until you unlock.
  • Slide to unlock and the lock screen camera work as before.
