Flappy Hats

Flappy Bird with hats, skins and your own sounds.

How to Use

  1. Open Flappy Hats and tap SHOP on the title screen.
  2. In HATS or SKINS, tap an item to preview it, then tap EQUIP or BUY. DEFAULT at the top is the stock look.
  3. Press and hold the cat ears or the cat skin to pick one of its colours.
  4. Score Rewards unlock as your best score goes up. Every game adds your score to your points.
  5. In SOUND, pick FLAP, POINT or GAME OVER, then hold HOLD TO RECORD (up to 2 seconds). Tap PLAY to hear it and USE to keep it. RESET goes back to the stock sound.
  6. Drag VOLUME to set that sound's loudness, from 0 to 200%. On FLAP, VIBRATE turns the flap tick on or off.

What it does

Flappy Bird 1.1.0 with a shop on the title screen. Unlock hats and skins with your best score or buy them with points, and record your own sounds.

  • Tap SHOP on the title screen for 37 hats and 49 skins, previewed on the bird. Press and hold the cat ears or the cat skin for 11 colours.
  • Score Rewards unlock as your best score goes up. The rest cost points: every game earns points equal to your score.
  • Shares your best score with every Flappy Bird on the phone (the App Store one, Flappy 60FPS and Hats 60FPS), so rewards unlock right away.
  • Record your own flap, point and game over sounds, set each one's volume, and turn on a tick for every flap.
  • Shares your points, items, colours and sounds with Hats 60FPS: change something in one and it's in the other.
  • Installs as its own app, Flappy Hats, next to the original. Game Center won't recognize it, and there are no ads.
