Message GIFs

GIPHY GIFs in Messages.

Install

Open in Cydia Add slopkit to Cydia

How to Use

  1. Install TLSFix from Cydia, then open tlsroot.litten.ca in Safari and install its certificates.
  2. Make a free API key at developers.giphy.com (Create an App, choose API) and copy it.
  3. In Messages, touch and hold the camera button and tap Paste Key, or enter the key in Settings › Message GIFs.
  4. Touch and hold the camera button, find a GIF, and tap it to add it to your message.

What it does

  • Touch and hold the camera button in a conversation to open the GIF picker. A quick tap still opens the usual photo menu.
  • Search GIPHY, or browse Trending and quick picks like Reactions, LOL, Love and Thank You.
  • Tap a GIF to add it to your message, then send it as usual.
  • Recents keeps the last 30 GIFs you sent, even offline.
  • Needs your own free GIPHY API key from developers.giphy.com, plus TLSFix and its certificates from tlsroot.litten.ca.

Settings

Without slopkit Settings installed, it's at Settings › Message GIFs.
