What it does

  • Tap compose in Messages and pick someone you've texted before: your conversation with them shows under the To: field right away, like iOS 7.
  • Add more people and it switches to that group's conversation, or goes blank if there isn't one.
  • Only looks, never creates a conversation. Send, drafts and Cancel work as usual, and no typing indicator is sent until you send.
  • Sending keeps the conversation on screen, and with MessageWallpapers the chat's wallpaper shows too.
