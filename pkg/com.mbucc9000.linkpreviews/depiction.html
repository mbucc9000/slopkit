What it does

  • A link in a message gets a card with the page's picture, title and site, right above the bubble.
  • Tap the card to open the link, or touch and hold it to copy it.
  • Everything loads on this iPhone, straight from each website. No server.
  • For modern HTTPS sites, install TLSFix and its certificates from tlsroot.litten.ca.
