BannerSwipe

Swipe banners up to dismiss them.

How to Use

  1. When a banner shows up, swipe it up to dismiss it.
  2. Turn it off or on in Settings > BannerSwipe.

What it does

  • Swipe a notification banner up and it flies off the top, like on newer iOS.
  • It stays in Notification Center, just like a banner that timed out.
  • Tapping a banner and pulling it down into Notification Center work as before.
  • Can be turned off in Settings, no respring needed.
