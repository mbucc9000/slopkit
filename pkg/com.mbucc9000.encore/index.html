What it does

An unofficial Spotify client for iOS 6, which also runs on iOS 5. Music streams from your own Encore Bridge, which you run on a computer at home (Windows or Docker). Needs Spotify Premium. Not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Spotify.

  • Home, Your Library, playlists, albums, artists, search and Liked Songs, in green iOS 6 style.
  • Now Playing with swipe-to-skip, Up Next, synced lyrics and a Listening-on bar.
  • Spotify Jam, and profiles: search for people and open their playlists.
  • Plays in the background, with lock screen controls and AirPods.
  • Anonymous Mode hides names and pictures when you show the app off.
  • Works on iPhone 4, 4S and 5, and iPod touch, on iOS 5 and 6.
