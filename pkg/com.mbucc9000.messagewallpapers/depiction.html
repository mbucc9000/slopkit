What it does

  • Press and hold a chat's background, or its title, to pick a wallpaper.
  • 12 presets are included, or use any photo.
  • On a wallpapered chat the entry bar goes away: the camera, a clean rounded text box and Send float on the wallpaper.
  • A Text Box slider sets how see-through the box is.
  • Timestamps and Delivered turn white with a shadow, so they stay readable.
