QR Scanner

A simple QR code scanner.

How to Use

  1. Open QR Scanner and point the camera at a QR code.
  2. When it's read, tap the card's action (Open in Safari, Call, Send Email and so on) or Copy. Done goes back to scanning.
  3. To read a code in a picture, tap Photos and pick it. In the dark, tap Light.

What it does

Point the camera at a QR code and it's read right away. Open the link, call the number, or copy the text or Wi-Fi password.

  • Opens straight to the camera and reads any QR code in view, at any angle.
  • Links open in Safari, phone numbers call, email and text codes open Mail or Messages, and map codes open Maps.
  • Wi-Fi codes show the network and its password, ready to copy.
  • Photos reads a QR code from a saved picture or screenshot.
  • Light turns on the flashlight. Tap the screen to focus.
  • Everything happens on this iPhone. No server.
