What it does

An unofficial Venmo client for iOS 6. It talks to Venmo straight from your phone; there's no server to run. Needs TLSFix (and its certificates) for modern HTTPS, and a computer once to copy your device ID. Venmo's terms don't allow unofficial apps and Venmo can limit accounts over it. Not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Venmo or PayPal.

  • Feed: your friends' payments and your own, with likes and comments.
  • Pay or Request: recent people, friends and your contacts show as you type.
  • Requests: pay, decline, remind or cancel. The tab badge counts the ones waiting on you.
  • Me: balance, transfer to bank, payment methods.
  • Every payment asks for your 4-digit Venn PIN.
  • A built-in setup guide walks you through TLSFix, the device ID and signing in.
