What it does

  • Type a message, touch and hold Send, and pick a time.
  • Your iPhone sends it then, even if Messages is closed or the phone is asleep.
  • Touch and hold Send again to see what's scheduled: send it now, change the time or delete it.
  • Works with iMessage, SMS and group chats. Plain text only.
