What it does

  • A light tap on every key press, with adjustable strength and length.
  • Hold the space bar and slide to move the cursor, iOS 12 style, with a tick as it moves.
  • Swipe typing: slide across a word's letters and lift. A trail follows your finger, a preview shows the word, and holding brings up alternatives.
  • Delete right after a swipe removes the whole word.
