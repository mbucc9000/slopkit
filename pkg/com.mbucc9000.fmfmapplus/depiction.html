FMF Map Plus

Satellite view and name tags in Find My Friends.

How to Use

  1. Open Find My Friends. The map starts in Satellite.
  2. On a full-screen map (tap the small map on the All list, or open a friend), tap the button in the bottom-left corner to switch between Standard, Satellite and Hybrid.
  3. Names show above everyone's dots. Tap a dot for the full details, as before.

There are no settings to configure.

What it does

  • The map opens in Satellite, and a button in its corner switches between Standard, Satellite and Hybrid.
  • Every friend gets a little name tag above their dot, so you can see who is who without tapping.
  • Two friends with the same first name get a last initial.
  • Satellite uses the same imagery as the Maps app, so it needs MapsX.
